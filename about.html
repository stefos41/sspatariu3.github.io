About & Goals

Below you will find a glimpse into my story, pitch, and goals for my career as an engineer striving to solve real-world problems and provide solutions to benefit society.

Biography

  1. 01

    Where I Come From

    I grew up in the Atlanta area, always passionate about understanding how devices work. My first real spark was when I was questioning how it was possible for an iPhone, while being so small, to become more and more like a computer. This led me to realize that our world revolves around how small we can make a transistor. With this in mind, I was driven to pursue a degree in engineering.

  2. 02

    A Defining Moment

    In the summer after freshman year, I wanted to take the time to enhance my skill set and make the most of the time I had to be more competitive in the job market for the upcoming semester, so I worked on making an AI voice-interactive fitness assistant. Everything was going smoothly, and I ended up getting the foundation working, but there seemed to be an inconvenience. The user experience was poor, as the application had a long, awkward lag during conversations. The solution was to reengineer the backend so that it used a streaming API to reduce the latency of waiting for a full response before providing any feedback. The result was instant. The lag was eliminated, which allowed the conversation to flow instantly. This showed me that I have to build for the person on the other side of the screen to create a truly successful product.

  3. 03

    What Drew Me to Computer Engineering

    Computer engineering was the epitome of what I was wondering about when it came to how devices work. Because it sits at the intersection of hardware and software, I believed it would be the best option to learn about the most influential advancements in our society. This led me to Georgia Tech, as it is a prime institution in this area and can help me better understand how to build for the person on the other side of the screen.

  4. 04

    What Drives Me

    I value getting things done for the benefit of whoever is using them. I believe that is our goal as engineers: to make the day-to-day things we do as people work just a little bit better each day. As time moves forward, these little improvements will exponentially change how our society works.

Elevator Pitch

My Pitch

Diligent Georgia Tech Computer Engineering student with proven experience delivering enterprise-ready software. Seeking an Embedded Systems internship starting May 2027 to contribute clean, scalable code and a proactive problem-solving approach to drive impactful solutions.

Career Goals

Long-Term Aspirations

My long-term goals would be to work on and understand how devices are able to be broken into, either physically or over the cloud, and preventively mitigate these intrusions. Roles such as embedded, hardware, or software security engineer would be interesting to take on, as the work is influential to society.

How I'll Get There

  1. 1

    Courses & Threads

    Threads in Cybersecurity and Signals Information Processing would allow me to take courses such as Reverse Malware Engineering. This will help me understand the ins and outs of how embedded devices can be broken into and secured.

  2. 2

    Skills to Build

    I haven't worked with a real-time operating system or read datasheets under time pressure. I'll close that gap by taking an embedded systems course and building one project that uses an RTOS.

  3. 3

    Internships, Co-ops & Research

    I am looking to do a summer internship in 2027 and, right after, either a fall or spring internship. Companies such as Tesla and Lockheed Martin are constantly looking for engineers to protect their systems.

  4. 4

    Clubs & Organizations

    I am involved in a Cybersecurity VIP related to learning the ins and outs of what is needed for DoD compliance in terms of cybersecurity. I hope to understand the latest controls and practices needed to have a compliant system in place, allowing me to bring a framework of security to my workplace.
